## 4. Chiqarish tartibi

1. **Release → Setup → App signing:** Play App Signing majburiy.
   - Hozir APK'ni GitHub'dan o'rnatgan mijozlar ko'p bo'lsa, **"Use existing app signing key"** ni tanlang va CI keystore'ni PEPK vositasi bilan yuklang. Shunda Play'dagi yangilanish ularning ilovasi ustiga o'rnatiladi.
   - Aks holda kalitni Google yaratsin, CI keystore esa *upload key* bo'lib qoladi (yo'qolsa, Play orqali tiklanadi). GitHub'dan o'rnatilgan APK ustiga Play versiyasi o'rnatilmaydi: avval eskisini o'chirish kerak. Ma'lumotlar serverda, qayta kirishning o'zi yetadi.
2. **AAB:** `main`ga merge qilingandan keyin "Android APK" workflow `apk-v1.0.N` Release'ini yaratadi. Undan `Avto-A1-1.0.N-play.aab` ni oling. `versionCode` = workflow run raqami, har yangi yuklashda o'zi oshadi.
3. **Target API:** yangi ilova va yangilanishlar API 36 (Android 16) ni nishonga olishi shart. Loyihada `targetSdkVersion = 36` — talab bajarilgan.
4. **Closed testing:** 13-noyabr 2023 dan keyin ochilgan *shaxsiy* hisob uchun Production'dan oldin kamida **12 tester 14 kun uzluksiz** closed test'da bo'lishi kerak. Tashkilot hisobiga bu talab yo'q. Testerlar Google akkaunti bilan opt-in havolasidan qo'shilib, ilovani o'rnatishi kerak.
5. **Production:** 14 kundan keyin "Apply for production" → savollarga javob → yuborish.

Manbalar: [testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465), [target API](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878).
